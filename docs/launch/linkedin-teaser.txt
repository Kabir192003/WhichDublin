For a while now, I've been quietly building something for a problem every student in Dublin knows too well.

Finding a place to live here isn't just expensive. It's 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗳𝘂𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗴.

You open a listing site to see a flat.
You open Google Maps to check the commute.
You Google "average rent Rathmines".
You ask Reddit if €950 for a room is fair.
You guess what bills and groceries will cost.
You wonder if the landlord is even real.

Six tabs. Three apps. And still no clear answer to the only question that matters:

"𝗖𝗮𝗻 𝗜 𝗮𝗰𝘁𝘂𝗮𝗹𝗹𝘆 𝗮𝗳𝗳𝗼𝗿𝗱 𝘁𝗼 𝗹𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗵𝗲𝗿𝗲, 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗵𝗼𝘄 𝗹𝗼𝗻𝗴 𝘄𝗶𝗹𝗹 𝗶𝘁 𝘁𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝗺𝗲 𝘁𝗼 𝗴𝗲𝘁 𝘁𝗼 𝗰𝗼𝗹𝗹𝗲𝗴𝗲?"

Listing apps show you a flat. Journey planners show you one trip. Cost-of-living guides give you a national average.

𝗡𝗼 𝗮𝗽𝗽 𝗽𝘂𝘁𝘀 𝗶𝘁 𝗮𝗹𝗹 𝘁𝗼𝗴𝗲𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗿 𝗶𝗻 𝗼𝗻𝗲 𝗽𝗹𝗮𝗰𝗲.

So I built one.

🏙️ 𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗿𝗼𝗱𝘂𝗰𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗪𝗵𝗶𝗰𝗵 𝗗𝘂𝗯𝗹𝗶𝗻. 𝗟𝗮𝘂𝗻𝗰𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘀𝗼𝗼𝗻.

One place for rent, living costs and travel time, for any area in Dublin.

📍 𝗘𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗿 𝗮𝗻𝘆 𝗘𝗶𝗿𝗰𝗼𝗱𝗲. Google Maps pins the exact address. Then you see real walking routes and journeys to every college, the nearest Lidl, Aldi and Tesco, part-time work nearby and typical rents. Every route opens in Google Maps.

🏠 𝗥𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 𝘁𝘆𝗽𝗲 𝗼𝗳 𝗵𝗼𝗺𝗲 across 106 areas, with the source on every number.

🚌 𝗟𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗯𝘂𝘀 𝗱𝗲𝗹𝗮𝘆𝘀, 𝗿𝗶𝗴𝗵𝘁 𝗻𝗼𝘄, plus timetabled journeys at 8am, late at night and on weekends.

💶 𝗬𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗹 𝗺𝗼𝗻𝘁𝗵𝗹𝘆 𝗰𝗼𝘀𝘁: rent + bills + travel + groceries + phone, adjusted for the home's energy rating.

🚩 𝗦𝗰𝗮𝗺 𝗰𝗵𝗲𝗰𝗸𝘀, 𝗱𝗲𝗽𝗼𝘀𝗶𝘁 𝗿𝘂𝗹𝗲𝘀 and a first-weeks checklist: IRP, Leap, bank, SIM.

💼 𝗣𝗮𝗿𝘁-𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗲 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸: wages, Stamp 2 hour limits and where the jobs are.

💚 𝗔 𝗻𝗼𝘁𝗲 𝗼𝗻 𝗸𝗲𝗲𝗽𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗶𝘁 𝗳𝗿𝗲𝗲

I've plugged in Google's Maps APIs so Eircode lookups and walking routes are as accurate as possible. Those APIs cost money beyond a free monthly allowance, and I want this to stay 𝗳𝗿𝗲𝗲 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 𝘀𝘁𝘂𝗱𝗲𝗻𝘁.

So I've capped usage inside that allowance. If the daily limit is reached, the app quietly switches back to open data (OpenStreetMap, NTA timetables, RTB rents) and keeps working.

𝗡𝗼𝗯𝗼𝗱𝘆 𝗴𝗲𝘁𝘀 𝗮 𝗽𝗮𝘆𝘄𝗮𝗹𝗹. 𝗡𝗼𝗯𝗼𝗱𝘆 𝗴𝗲𝘁𝘀 𝗮𝗱𝘀.

I'm not building this to sell anything. I'm building it because I know what it's like to land here and figure it all out alone, and 𝗻𝗼 𝘀𝘁𝘂𝗱𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝘀𝗵𝗼𝘂𝗹𝗱 𝗵𝗮𝘃𝗲 𝘁𝗼.

It's for incoming and existing students, and really for anyone renting in Dublin who wants the full picture before they sign.
